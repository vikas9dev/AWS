## 1. AWS Certified Solutions Architect Associate Exam Guide Overview

Let's review the [AWS Certified Solutions Architect Associate exam guide](https://d1.awsstatic.com/onedam/marketing-channels/website/aws/en_US/certification/approved/pdfs/docs-sa-assoc/AWS-Certified-Solutions-Architect-Associate_Exam-Guide.pdf) to understand what's expected for the exam. This guide is a comprehensive document outlining the knowledge areas and services that might be covered.

📝 **Note:** This course is designed to cover the topics most relevant to the exam. If a service is listed in the exam guide but not covered in the course, don't worry too much. If feedback indicates it's frequently on the exam, it will be added to the course.

### Exam Prerequisites and Format

- **Experience:** AWS recommends one year of hands-on experience, but this course should provide sufficient preparation.
- **Question Types:**
  - Multiple Choice: One correct answer out of four options.
  - Multiple Response: Two or more correct answers out of five options.
- **Answering Questions:** You must answer every question. There is no penalty for guessing. 💡 **Tip:** Always provide an answer, even if you're unsure.
- **Unscored Questions:** The exam includes 15 unscored questions used by AWS to evaluate new questions. You won't know which questions are unscored.
- **Scoring:** You are scored on 50 out of 65 questions.
- **Score Range:** 100 to 1000.
- **Passing Score:** 720.

### Exam Content Domains

The exam covers four domains. Your final results will indicate whether you passed each domain, but not your specific score per domain or question.

1.  **Designing Secure Architectures (30%)**: Security is a critical aspect of the exam.
2.  **Designing Resilient Architectures (26%)**: Focuses on high availability and fault tolerance.
3.  **Designing High-Performing Architectures (24%)**: Choosing the best services and configurations for optimal performance.
4.  **Designing Cost-Optimized Architectures (20%)**: Strategies for saving costs in the cloud.

### Domain Details

Review each domain's specific knowledge areas and tasks in the exam guide. The course is designed to cover these areas thoroughly.

- If you encounter a topic you're unfamiliar with, revisit the relevant course lectures.
- Consider consulting the official AWS documentation for more in-depth information.

### Appendix: Services Covered

The appendix lists services that _might_ appear on the exam.

- The course focuses on the most important and frequently tested services.
- If you encounter a question on a service not covered in the course, please provide feedback so the course can be updated.
- It's unlikely that a single uncovered question will prevent you from passing the exam. The goal is a high score!

## 2. Exam Tips 🚀

To maximize your chances of passing the exam, consider these tips:

1.  **Practice Makes Perfect**: 🏋️

    - If you're new to AWS and feel overwhelmed, dedicate time to AWS practice using this course.
    - Don't rush into the exam. Take your time to ensure you understand AWS concepts.
    - The exam recommends one or more years of hands-on AWS experience. You can't fake it!
    - Hands-on experience is crucial for learning AWS effectively.
    - If overwhelmed, review the course material again. It's okay to take your time.
    - Even experienced professionals revisit material, so don't hesitate to review.

2.  **Question Answering Strategies**: 🤔

    - **Proceed by Elimination**: Most questions are scenario-based and may be lengthy.
      - First, rule out answers you know are incorrect.
      - Then, choose the answer that makes the most sense.
    - **Don't Overthink**: There are very few trick questions.
      - If an answer seems feasible but is overly complicated, it's likely wrong.
    - **Flag for Review**: If you don't know the answer immediately:
      - Answer the question.
      - Flag it for review later using the exam's flag feature.
      - Revisit it later after you've had time to think.

3.  **AWS Whitepapers**: 📚

    - Skim through these AWS whitepapers:
      - [Architecting for the Cloud: AWS Best Practices](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/wellarchitected/latest/framework/welcome.html)
      - [AWS Well-Architected Framework](https://aws.amazon.com/blogs/aws/aws-well-architected-framework-updated-white-papers-tools-and-best-practices/)
      - [AWS Disaster Recovery](https://aws.amazon.com/disaster-recovery/)
    - These whitepapers can reinforce your learning and improve your understanding of AWS.

4.  **Service FAQs**: ❓

    - If you're unsure about a service, check its FAQ.
    - Most exam questions are based on the information in the FAQs.
    - 📌 **Example:** For VPC, the FAQ URL is `amazon.com/vpc/faqs`.
    - FAQs help ensure you thoroughly understand a service.

5.  **Engage with the AWS Community**: 🤝
    - Participate in this course by answering questions and helping others.
    - Review questions from other learners to identify and address your own doubts.
    - Complete the practice test in this section to assess your readiness.
    - Explore online forums, blogs, and local meetups to connect with other AWS engineers.
    - Watch AWS re:Invent videos on YouTube for valuable insights.

## 4. Scheduling Your AWS Certification Exam

Here's how to schedule your AWS certification exam:

1.  **Sign into your AWS Certification account:**

    - Go to: `aws.training/certification`
    - Click on "Sign In".
    - Create an AWS Builder ID if you don't already have one. This is a specific account for AWS certifications.

2.  **Access the Certification Portal:**

    - After signing in, click on "Go to Your Account". This will take you to the certification portal.

3.  **Exam Registration:**

    - Click on "Exam Registration" and then "Schedule an exam".
    - You'll see a list of exams you can take. Some may require authorization before scheduling.
    - Click on "Authorize" (if needed) and then "Schedule" for the exam you want to take. 📌 **Example:** AWS Certified Developer Associate Exam.

4.  **Pearson VUE Portal:**

    - Clicking "Schedule" will redirect you to the Pearson VUE portal, AWS's exam scheduling partner.

5.  **Choose Your Exam Delivery Method:**

    - **In person at a test center:**
      - Select "In person at a test center".
      - Bring a photo ID.
      - Understand the rules about what you can and cannot bring into the test center.
      - Select your language.
      - Enter an address to find nearby test centers.
    - **Online with OnVUE:** 💻
      - Select "Online with OnVUE".
      - You'll need a computer with a webcam and a good internet connection.
      - 💡 **Tip:** Run the system test _well in advance_ of your exam to ensure everything works correctly.
      - Your testing space must be clear of everything. Remove all items from your desk.
      - Bring an ID.
      - Check in about 30 minutes before your appointment time.
      - Choose your language.
      - Agree to the terms and conditions.
      - Select the language you want the proctor (who checks your desk, etc.) to speak. This is different from the exam language.
      - Select your time zone.
      - Select a date and time.
      - Complete the payment.
      - Add a voucher if you have a discount.

6.  **Launching Your Exam:**
    - Once scheduled, you can launch the exam through the Pearson VUE portal.
    - Go to "Home" then "Dashboard".
    - Click on "Manage my Pearson VUE account".
    - You should see a "Launch exam" button when it's time to start.

📝 **Note:** After scheduling, the exam might appear under "Upcoming Exams".

⚠️ **Warning:** Make sure to clear your desk and testing area completely if taking the exam online. The proctor will ask you to show your surroundings.

📌 **Example:** If you have a discount voucher, you can add it during the payment process.

💡 **Tip:** Watch the videos on exam accommodations (for extra time if you're not a native English speaker) and certification benefits (for potential discounts on future exams).

## 5. AWS Exam Discount Trick

Here's a trick to potentially save 💰 on your AWS exams! If you've passed an AWS exam before, you might be eligible for a 50% discount on your next one.

Here's how it works:

- After successfully passing an AWS exam, you'll receive tokens. These tokens can be redeemed for a 50% discount on your next AWS exam.
- Unclaimed tokens will be marked as such, along with an expiry date.

To claim your discount:

1.  Click on "Claim Benefits."
2.  Once claimed, the benefit will be listed as "claimed."
3.  You'll then receive a voucher code. 📝 **Note:** This code is what you'll use to get the 50% discount.

Applying the voucher code:

1.  When you're ready to pay for your next exam, proceed to checkout.
2.  You'll be prompted to enter payment information.
3.  Look for a box labeled "voucher promo code." This is where you'll enter the code you obtained earlier.
4.  Paste the voucher code into the box.
5.  The discount will then be applied to your total cost. 🎉

📌 **Example:**

```
Voucher Code: AWS-EXAM-DISCOUNT-123
```

Entering this code at checkout will reduce the exam price by 50%.

💡 **Tip:** Keep an eye on the expiry dates of your unclaimed benefits to avoid losing out on the discount.

I hope this helps you save money on your AWS certifications! Good luck! 🚀

## 6. Exam Information

Here's a breakdown of what to expect during the exam:

- First, you need to register online.
- Then, pay the \$150 exam fee. 💰
- On the day of the exam, you must provide a valid identity document, such as your ID or passport. All details will be sent to you via email after registration. 📧

During the exam:

- You are not allowed to take any notes. 📝
- You cannot use a pen. 🖊️
- Speaking is prohibited. 🤫
- If taking the exam at home, you'll need to scan your room to ensure it's completely empty. 🧹

Exam Details:

- The exam consists of 65 questions. ❓
- You will have 130 minutes to complete the exam. ⏱️
- If you're unsure about a question, you can use the flag feature to mark it for review later. 🚩

Reviewing and Submitting:

- At the end of the exam, you can review all questions and easily navigate back to the flagged ones.
- Once you submit the exam, you **cannot** change any of your responses. ⚠️

Passing the Exam:

- To pass, you need a score of at least 720 out of 1000. ✅
- You'll receive your results within five days. Usually, it's sooner, but it's not immediate. ⏳
- You will receive an email notification with instructions on how to download your score from the certification website. 📧
- You will receive your overall score a few days later.

Important Information:

- You will **never** know which answers were correct or incorrect. This is to prevent cheating. 🙅‍♀️
- If you fail, you can retake the exam after 14 days. 🗓️

Final Thoughts:

- Good luck with your exam! 👍
- Practice, practice, practice! 🏋️‍♀️
- Revisit the lectures in this course if you have any doubts. 📚
