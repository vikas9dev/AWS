# AWS Exam Preparation - Handnote 📝

## 📚 EXAM OVERVIEW

### AWS Certified Solutions Architect Associate (SAA-C03)
- **Experience Recommended**: 1 year hands-on (course provides sufficient prep)
- **Question Types**:
  - **Multiple Choice**: 1 correct answer out of 4
  - **Multiple Response**: 2+ correct answers out of 5
- **Total Questions**: 65 (50 scored, 15 unscored)
- **Time Limit**: 130 minutes
- **Passing Score**: 720 out of 1000
- **No Penalty**: Always answer every question (even if guessing)

---

## 📊 EXAM CONTENT DOMAINS

### Domain Breakdown
1. **Designing Secure Architectures (30%)** 🔒
   - Security is critical
   - IAM, encryption, network security

2. **Designing Resilient Architectures (26%)** 🛡️
   - High availability
   - Fault tolerance
   - Disaster recovery

3. **Designing High-Performing Architectures (24%)** ⚡
   - Optimal service selection
   - Performance optimization
   - Caching strategies

4. **Designing Cost-Optimized Architectures (20%)** 💰
   - Cost optimization
   - Right-sizing
   - Reserved instances, spot instances

## 🚀 EXAM TIPS

### 1. Practice Makes Perfect 🏋️
- ✅ Don't rush - take time to understand concepts
- ✅ Hands-on experience is crucial
- ✅ Review course material multiple times if needed
- ✅ Practice with AWS console

### 2. Question Answering Strategies 🤔
- ✅ **Elimination Method**: Rule out wrong answers first
- ✅ **Don't Overthink**: Few trick questions, choose simplest answer
- ✅ **Flag for Review**: Answer first, flag uncertain questions
- ✅ **Scenario-Based**: Most questions are lengthy scenarios

### 3. Study Resources 📚
- ✅ **AWS Whitepapers**:
  - AWS Well-Architected Framework
  - AWS Disaster Recovery
  - Architecting for the Cloud
- ✅ **Service FAQs**: Most exam questions based on FAQs
  - Format: `amazon.com/[service]/faqs`
- ✅ **AWS re:Invent Videos**: YouTube for insights

### 4. Community Engagement 🤝
- ✅ Answer questions in course
- ✅ Help other learners
- ✅ Online forums and blogs
- ✅ Local AWS meetups

## 📅 SCHEDULING YOUR EXAM

### Steps to Schedule
1. **Sign In**: `aws.training/certification`
2. **Create AWS Builder ID**: If needed
3. **Go to Certification Portal**: "Go to Your Account"
4. **Exam Registration**: "Schedule an exam"
5. **Authorize** (if needed) → **Schedule**

### Exam Delivery Options

#### In-Person at Test Center
- ✅ Bring photo ID
- ✅ Follow test center rules
- ✅ Select language
- ✅ Find nearby test centers

#### Online with OnVUE 💻
- ✅ Computer with webcam
- ✅ Good internet connection
- ✅ **System Test**: Run well in advance
- ✅ Clear desk (remove everything)
- ✅ Check in 30 minutes early
- ✅ ID required

## 📝 EXAM DAY INFORMATION

### Before Exam
- ✅ Register online
- ✅ Pay $150 exam fee
- ✅ Bring valid ID (passport/ID card)
- ✅ Email confirmation received

### During Exam
- ❌ **No notes** allowed
- ❌ **No pen** allowed
- ❌ **No speaking** allowed
- ✅ **Flag questions** for review
- ✅ **130 minutes** total time
- ✅ **65 questions** total

### After Exam
- ✅ **Review**: All questions before submitting
- ✅ **Submit**: Cannot change answers after
- ✅ **Results**: Within 5 days (usually sooner)
- ✅ **Email**: Notification with score download
- ✅ **Passing**: 720/1000 minimum

### Important Notes
- ⚠️ **Never know correct answers** (prevents cheating)
- ⚠️ **Retake**: After 14 days if failed
- ⚠️ **Domain Results**: Pass/fail per domain (not scores)
